Our goal is to provide you with the best experience possible. We create unforgettable whitewater adventures with experienced guides, beautiful scenery, and a strong commitment to safety. Oars up!

History

Rapids started floating the river in 1995. For more than 30 years, local guides have led families through safe, exciting whitewater.

Today we are still a family-owned company. Our goal is the same: skilled guides, beautiful rivers, and memories that last.
