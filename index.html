Limón

Limón is a vibrant province on Costa Rica's Caribbean coast, celebrated for its rich Afro-Caribbean heritage, tropical rainforests, and laid-back beach towns. Unlike the rest of the country, its distinct culture shines through calypso music, colorful architecture, and flavorful coconut-infused cuisine. Top highlights include Tortuguero National Park, famous for its sea turtle nesting sites and scenic jungle canals, and Cahuita National Park, known for white-sand beaches and coral reefs. Fun-loving coastal hubs like Puerto Viejo offer world-class surfing, wildlife spotting, and a relaxed reggae vibe, making Limón a top destination for nature lovers and cultural explorers.
